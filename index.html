          Thermo Glossary, Quick Reference
          Isentropic Constant entropy, an idealized, reversible, adiabatic process. Real compressors/turbines fall short (see the Efficiency sliders).
          Adiabatic No heat transfer in or out. Compression and expansion strokes are modeled as adiabatic; only the heat-addition/rejection steps transfer heat.
          Entropy, s A measure of disorder / heat-transfer potential. Plotted on the horizontal axis of every T-s diagram in this app.
          Enthalpy, h Internal energy plus flow work (h = u + Pv). Used directly for the vapor cycles (Rankine, refrigeration) via the steam tables.
          Thermal Efficiency η = Wnet/Qin for a heat engine, the fraction of heat input converted to useful work.
          COP Coefficient of Performance = Q/Win for refrigeration/heat pumps. Not capped at 1, values above 1 are normal and expected.
          Carnot Limit 1 − TL/TH, the maximum efficiency any heat engine can achieve between two temperatures. No real cycle can exceed it (2nd law).
          Cold-Air-Standard This app's gas-cycle assumption: constant cp, cv, γ (air properties), no combustion chemistry. Standard for teaching, not exact for real gas turbines.
          Saturation The T/P at which a fluid coexists as liquid and vapor together. The vapor cycles interpolate a saturation table for this.
          Superheat Heating vapor beyond its saturation temperature at a given pressure, keeps turbine-exit steam drier (less blade erosion).
          Regeneration Reusing waste heat internally (e.g. hot turbine exhaust preheating compressor exit air) to cut fuel use without adding heat-source temperature.
          Infeasible Point A slider combination with no physical solution (e.g. real compression already exceeds the max cycle temperature). TAS flags these instead of showing a bad number.
